Subscription terms

How yearly packages work.

The terms for our yearly packages, in plain English. Your quote and these terms make up our agreement. Last updated 1 November 2026.

What you're paying for

A yearly package is a 12-month licence to use the website or system we build for you, such as a POS system or an online shop. Each renewal gives you another 12 months.

Zenatiq owns the code. You own your data: your products, customers, sales and anything else you put in.

If you chose a one-time purchase instead, you get free updates and help for 2 months after it goes live. After that, updates, changes and help are charged separately, and we always quote first. The rest of this page is about yearly packages.

What the yearly fee includes

  • Hosting, so your website or system stays online.
  • Updates and fixes that keep it working and secure.
  • Small updates and changes during the year.
  • Backups of your data.
  • Help on WhatsApp and by phone.

New features, extra pages and bigger changes aren't included, and we'll always quote for them first. Anything paid to other companies, such as SMS credits or a paid domain name, is listed in your quote.

Your price

Your quote shows two prices: the first year, and the yearly renewal after that. Each renewal is due by the last day of the year you've paid for.

Renewing

  • We email you 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before your package is due, with a link to your own renewal page.
  • You can renew for one, two or three years at a time.
  • Pay online through PayHere, or by bank transfer using the details on your renewal page. Online payments go through straight away, and bank transfers as soon as we've received them.
  • Renewing early never costs you any days: each renewal adds to the date you're already paid until.

If you don't renew

  • 14 days' grace. For 14 days after your package ends, everything keeps working, so there's time to renew.
  • Then read-only. After those 14 days, your system becomes read-only. You can still sign in, look things up and export your data, but you can't add or change anything. Renew at any time and everything switches back on.
  • Your data for 90 more days. We keep your data for 90 days after your system becomes read-only, so you can export it or come back. After that, it's deleted for good.

If you need help exporting your data, message us and we'll help.

Stopping

You can stop at any time: tell us and we'll stop the reminders. Your package keeps running until the end of the year you've paid for, and then the steps above apply.

Price changes

If your renewal price is going to change, we'll tell you at least 30 days before your renewal is due. A year you've already paid for never costs more.

Refunds

Once a paid year has started, it can't be refunded. You can stop renewing at the end of any year.
