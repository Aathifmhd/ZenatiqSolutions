Questions

Ask a question

Is the mock-up really free?

Yes. We design a preview of your website so you can see exactly what you'd get. If you don't love it, you don't pay anything and there's no obligation.

How much does a website cost?

Websites and online shops start from Rs 15,000, and POS systems from Rs 40,000. The final price depends on the pages and features you need, and you'll know it before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Your free mock-up is usually ready in a few days. The build time depends on the size of the project, and you'll get a timeline together with your price.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. With a care plan from Rs 3,500 a month, we look after your hosting, domain and business email and handle the monthly maintenance, so you can focus on your business.

Do you only work with businesses in Kandy?

We're based in Kandy, but most of the work happens over WhatsApp and calls, so we can help businesses anywhere in Sri Lanka.

What do I need to get started?

Just a message. If you have them, send your logo, a few photos of your shop or work, your main products or services, and your opening hours and location.
