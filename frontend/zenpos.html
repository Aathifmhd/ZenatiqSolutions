How it works

From free trial to licence.

  1. Try it free 14 DAYS

    Use ZenPOS on your shop computer for 14 days before you pay anything.

  2. Send your details

    Fill in the form above, with the Computer ID from ZenPOS → Subscription.

  3. Pay for the year

    Rs 15,000 for one computer, online or by bank transfer.

  4. Activate

    Paste the licence key we send you in ZenPOS → Subscription. Internet is needed once.

Terms

  • Yearly subscription: LKR 15,000 a year for one computer, with service and small updates for the whole year.
  • Subscription payments are not refundable.
  • Renewing early adds the new year after the current one.
  • After the paid-until date there are 14 days to renew. After that ZenPOS is read-only until it's renewed; the shop's data stays safe and can still be viewed, printed and backed up.
  • Bigger changes and new features are quoted separately.
