Regal Hardware: one system for five tills and an online shop.

A busy hardware store needed billing that could keep up across several counters, an online shop customers could order from, and one place to see sales, stock and staff.

What we built

  • An Odoo-based POS on five terminals
  • A custom ERP for stock, suppliers & accounts
  • An online store whose orders flow straight into the ERP
  • Customer sign-in by mobile number & SMS code
  • SMS bills for customers
  • Quotes, cheques, loyalty points & gift vouchers
  • Fingerprint attendance & payroll
  • Old records migrated & invoices redesigned
