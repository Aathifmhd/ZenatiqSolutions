About Zenatiq

A designer's eye. An engineer's discipline.

Zenatiq Solutions is a founder-led studio in Kandy, Sri Lanka. It's run by Mohamed Aathif, a UX designer and software engineer who designs and builds every project personally.

Why Zenatiq exists

Too many local businesses get stuck between two bad options: a cheap website that looks like everyone else's, or an agency that's expensive, slow and hard to reach.

Zenatiq is the third option: thoughtful design, solid engineering and honest prices, from one person you can actually talk to. You see a free mock-up before you pay, and after launch you message the same person who built it.

The work is shaped around how Sri Lankan businesses really run: customers on WhatsApp, bills by SMS, prices in rupees, and billing that keeps going when the internet drops.
