Privacy

Your details stay yours.

A plain-English explanation of what this website does, and doesn't do, with your information. Last updated 24 October 2026.

No cookies, no tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We don't follow you around the web.

The contact form

The form on our contact page doesn't send or store anything on our servers. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your answers are turned into a message that opens in WhatsApp on your own device, and you choose whether to send it.

Messages you send on WhatsApp are also covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you message or call us, we use your name, number and the details you share only to reply to you and to work on your project. We don't sell your details or share them for marketing.

If you'd like us to delete your messages or details, just ask on WhatsApp or call +94 75 811 2170.

Services this website uses

  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.
  • Firebase Hosting (by Google) serves the website and may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.
  • Links to other websites, such as the projects on our work page, lead to sites with their own privacy policies.
